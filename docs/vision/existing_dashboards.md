# Existing viewers/dashboards (reviewed 2026-11-10)

| Site | What it shows |
|---|---|
| WKP Dataviewer https://wkp.rws.nl/dataviewer/waterlichamen | Bars of water bodies by category/status/type; pie-tree of state per quality element (Ecologie totaal → biology / fys-chem / SVS; chemistry branch); bars of substances failing in ≥1% of bodies. Tabs also for pressures/impacts, measures, exemptions. Filters: plan period, level (NL / district / provincie / beheerder / water body), year |
| WKP Geoviewer https://wkp.rws.nl/geoviewer | National map; style by status / category / type / assessment; WMS from geo.rijkswaterstaat.nl |
| Haskoning – Schelde, Drenthe, Hollandse Delta (`royalhaskoningdhv.shinyapps.io`) | Same template: "bollenschema" (pie-tree), stacked bars of verdict per year, leaflet status map with popups + shapefile download. Drenthe adds doelgat (distance to goal N/P) map and "number of exceeding substances per cluster" map. Hollandse Delta adds expert pages (origin of verdict) |
| Haskoning – Viewer Rijn-Oost | Leaflet status map with RWZI and monitoring layers, doelgat, source analysis |
| Haskoning – KRW-tussenevaluatie | Pie comparison 2023 vs 2024, national/district/beheerder, prognosis 2027, groundwater |
| Haskoning – KRW-maatregelen | Pies/maps of measures expected by 2027 (data not in our folder) |
| Haskoning – WSBD | EKR trend per water body against coloured class bands; ecological key factors (ESF) |

**Gap we target:** all of these are descriptive (pie / stacked bar / one-colour map / EKR trend), one body or one year at a
time. None compares bodies against each other, explains *why*, tracks goals over time, contrasts absolute ecology with
goal-relative status, or compares countries.
